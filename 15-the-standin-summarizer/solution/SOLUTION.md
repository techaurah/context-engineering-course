# Solution: The stand-in summarizer: what a query-free summary quietly drops

The mulch fact returns ('five centimetres' is now a keep signal), taking the summary to 3 of 4 key facts at 95 tokens. The soil not leaves fact still drops: it carries no quantity at all, only an instruction, and no surface rule short of understanding will catch it. That residue is the honest limit of heuristic summarization, and the reason the capstone compresses with a query whenever one exists.
