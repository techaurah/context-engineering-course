# Solution: What context engineering is: the anatomy of a window

History roughly doubles (156 tokens at 6 turns, about 310 at 12, using the same conversation cycled), while the other components stay fixed, so history's share rises from 23.4 percent toward a third of the window and retrieval's share falls even though retrieval did not change. Lesson: shares are a property of the whole assembly. A component can become a problem purely by standing still while another grows, which is why Lesson 03 budgets components separately instead of budgeting the window as one number.
