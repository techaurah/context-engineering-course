# Solution: Lost in the middle: position effects, measured

The dip stays in the middle positions (5 and 6 of 10) because that is where the position weight bottoms out, but its depth varies: each fact carries its own seeded jitter of plus or minus 0.05, so one task's facts may sit at 0.55 while another's reach 0.75. The position effect sets the shape; the individual fact decides whether it clears the threshold on a given trial. That is why the course always reports recall over 20 trials rather than a single run.
