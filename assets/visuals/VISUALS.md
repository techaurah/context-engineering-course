# Visual guide

Every diagram is generated by `generate_visuals.py` in this
folder; rerun it to regenerate the set. Numbers on the charts are
the measured outputs of the lessons they cite.

- `course-map.svg`: the ten modules and their lesson ranges.
- `window-anatomy.svg`: Lesson 01's measured anatomy of one
  assembled context (retrieval 56 percent of 668 tokens).
- `u-curve.svg`: Lesson 05's position sweep, the U-shaped recall
  curve of the stand-in reader.
- `four-operations.svg`: write, select, compress, isolate, the
  LangChain taxonomy the middle modules are organised by.
- `budget-ledger.svg`: Lesson 03's budget ledger after 48 turns,
  over the window by 54 tokens.
- `compaction-strategies.svg`: Lesson 16's three history
  strategies, tokens against facts survived.
- `capstone-pipeline.svg`: the capstone pipeline and its gate
  history, failure included.
