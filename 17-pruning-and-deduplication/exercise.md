# Exercise: Pruning and deduplication: near copies, and the fact the short copy lost

Measure the pairwise Jaccard scores directly: the highest score between two genuinely different documents, and the lowest score between a document and its copy. Then argue where the threshold should sit on THIS corpus, and what would change your answer on a corpus with heavy shared boilerplate.

Work it with the lesson's code open beside you. Every number in the worked solution comes from running a variant of that code, and you should be able to reproduce each one.
