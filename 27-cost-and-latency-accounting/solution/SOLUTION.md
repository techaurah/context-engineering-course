# Solution: Cost accounting: read a run like a ledger

History replay's share climbs further (it grows quadratically in effect, since each step replays a longer history), while the compact tool saving, constant per step, shrinks as a share of the total. One sentence: the longer the run, the more its cost is a history policy problem and the less any per step optimisation matters, which is why compaction (Lesson 16) outranks result schemas (Lesson 25) for long running agents.
