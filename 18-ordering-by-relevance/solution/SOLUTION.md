# Solution: Ordering: what the edges strategy actually buys, measured on all facts

On required facts only, the picture flips or tightens dramatically, because the required facts live in the top ranked document, which every strategy seats well (first in desc and edges, last in asc, both good seats). The metric was the message all along: ordering advice that only tracks the hero fact will always flatter strategies that pamper it. A course, and a team, should decide whose facts count before arguing about order.
