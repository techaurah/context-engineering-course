# Solution: Prefix stability and caching: layout decisions with a price tag

Cached tokens fall to roughly the system segment alone per turn: the prefix matches through system, then meets a different document each turn and stops, so tools, memory, and everything later are billed fresh every turn despite being identical. Placement, not content, decides. The rule generalises beyond caching: early positions are the most expensive real estate in the window for anything volatile, in attention (Lesson 05) and in billing alike.
